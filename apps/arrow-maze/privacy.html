Privacy Policy

What happens to your information when you play Arrow Maze

Arrow Maze: Magic Puzzle ("the app") is made by Hasan Burak Koçyiğit ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

  • We do not collect, receive or share any personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app works completely offline and does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • your settings (language, sound, vibration, theme and accessibility options);
  • your progress (solved levels and your best results);
  • the level you are currently playing, so you can continue later.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase your progress at any time in Settings › Reset progress. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code and makes no network requests. We cannot see who plays the app or how.

Crash reports and device backups

If you have allowed it in your device settings, Apple (or Google on Android) may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. They do not identify you and are covered by Apple's or Google's own privacy policies. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings.

Your device's own backup (for example iCloud) may include the app's saved data, depending on your settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal data, we collect none from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete: the data on your device is fully under your control. You may still contact us with any question about your privacy, including under laws such as the GDPR or Turkey's personal data protection law (KVKK).

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that processes data (for example Game Center achievements), we will update this policy and the app's privacy information in the store before that version is released. The date below shows the latest version.
