Features

Everything you need to keep shared spending fair

👥

Groups for Anything

Create a group for a trip, a shared home, a couple or a one-off night out. Every expense stays in its own context with a running balance.

🧮

Split Any Way

Equal shares, exact amounts, percentages or custom shares. Edit an expense later and the original split intent is restored, not just the numbers.

✅

Settle Up Safely

Record a payment and the related debts close together. Made a mistake? Undo a settlement within 60 minutes, and balances roll straight back.

📷

Add Friends by QR

Scan a friend's personal QR code or pick them from your contacts. Friendships are mutual, so both sides see the same balance.

🧾

Attach Receipts

Snap a photo of the bill or pick one from your library. The receipt stays attached to the expense so nobody has to remember the details.

🔔

Live Activity Feed

New expenses, settlements and group changes appear in real time, with push notifications in your own language.
