Privacy Policy

Protecting your personal data matters to us

1. Overview

The Prayer Times: Adhan Alerts app (the "App") respects the privacy of its users. This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, how it is used and how it is protected.

2. Data We Collect

a) Location Data

To calculate prayer times accurately and determine the qibla direction, the App asks for permission to access your device's location (GPS coordinates). Your location data is used only for these purposes and is not stored on our servers; it is processed locally on your device.

b) Device Sensor Data

The qibla compass feature uses your device's magnetic sensor (compass) and orientation sensors. This data is processed in real time only to calculate the qibla direction and is not recorded.

c) User Preferences

User preferences such as notification settings, language preference and the selected city are stored only locally on your device (AsyncStorage). This information is not sent to our servers.

3. How We Use Data

The data collected is used for the following purposes:

  • Showing accurate prayer times for your location
  • Determining the qibla direction
  • Sending prayer time notifications on time
  • Remembering your app preferences
  • Improving the performance of the App

4. Third-Party Services

a) Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. For details, please see the Google Privacy Policy.

b) Apple App Store and Google Play Store

The App is distributed through the respective platform stores. The privacy policies of these platforms apply.

5. Data Security

The following measures are taken to keep your personal data secure:

  • Location data is processed locally on your device and is not transferred to a server
  • User preferences are stored only on your device
  • We never collect or store your payment information

6. Notifications

The App may send notifications to remind you of prayer times. Notification permission is requested on first use, and notifications can be turned on or off separately for each prayer time. Notifications are scheduled entirely locally on the device.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. Parents are encouraged to supervise their children's use of the App.

8. User Rights

As a user, you have the following rights:

  • You can revoke location permissions at any time from your device settings
  • You can turn off notifications
  • You can request a copy of the data stored about you

To exercise these rights, you can contact us at burakkocyigit1@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. If significant changes are made, you will be notified within the App. By continuing to use the App, you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For questions or requests about our privacy policy, you can contact us:

Email: burakkocyigit1@gmail.com
