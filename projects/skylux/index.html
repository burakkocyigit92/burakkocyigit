The challenge

Bookings lived in chat threads

Every booking started as a WhatsApp chat or a phone call. The team needed one place to run the business, without making booking any harder for the guests.

01

Requests got lost

Enquiries arrived by WhatsApp, phone and Instagram, with no single list to work from.

02

No record of quotes

Nobody could see which price had been sent to which guest, or when.

03

Planning by hand

Vehicles and drivers were matched to trips manually, so double bookings were easy to miss.

04

A developer for every change

Prices, routes and website texts could not be updated by the team itself.

05

Guests from everywhere

International guests expect to read and book in their own language.

The solution

One system with two faces

A public website for guests and an operations panel for the team. Both share one database, so nothing is typed twice.

Public website

A fast, premium site that turns visitors into quote requests.

  • Eight languages, including Arabic in right-to-left
  • Quote form for one-way and round trips; the return leg fills itself in reverse
  • Links can pre-fill the form with route, date, passengers and vehicle
  • Hand-off to WhatsApp with the request number
  • SEO built in: hreflang, sitemap and structured data

Operations panel

Everything the team needs, from the first message to the completed trip.

  • One inbox for every request, with quick filters and search
  • The route's price for every vehicle, and a quote in two clicks
  • Vehicle and driver assignment with conflict warnings
  • Ready-made WhatsApp messages in the guest's language
  • Content, photos, prices and fleet managed by the team
  • Roles for super admins, admins and operators

How it works

From first click to completed transfer

The whole booking flow runs through the panel, and every step leaves a trace in the request's history.

  1. Request

    A guest sends the form, or the team logs a WhatsApp or phone enquiry. Each request gets a number such as TRF-2026-000233.

  2. Quote

    The panel lists the route's price for every vehicle. The team picks one and sends the quote on WhatsApp, in the guest's language.

  3. Confirmation

    The guest confirms on WhatsApp and the request moves to Confirmed.

  4. Assignment

    Each leg gets a vehicle and a driver. Overlapping jobs are flagged without blocking the team.

  5. Transfer

    Driver details go to the guest in one click, and today's trips are on the dashboard.

  6. Completion

    When every leg is done, the request closes itself.

Under the hood

Details that make it production-ready

Multilingual by design

Interface texts in eight languages kept in sync by a test; content in the database with an English fallback.

Spam-protected forms

Cloudflare Turnstile, a honeypot field and rate limits per IP address and per phone number.

WhatsApp in eight languages

Quote, confirmation and driver messages are generated from the request, and opening one is logged.

Conflict-aware planning

Each transfer reserves its vehicle and driver for a set time; overlapping jobs are flagged before they happen.

Secure by default

Row-level security on every table, column-level permissions for staff and an audit trail of changes.

Privacy built in

KVKK/GDPR consent stored with time and policy version, and cookieless analytics, so no cookie banner.

No developer needed

Prices, locations, fleet, texts and photos are all managed by the team in the panel.

Self-hosted and backed up

Docker on Hetzner with Coolify, nightly database and media backups, health checks and uptime alerts.
