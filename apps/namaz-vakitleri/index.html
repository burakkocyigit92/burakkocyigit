Features

Designed to make your worship easier

🕐

Prayer Times

Follow imsak, sunrise, dhuhr, asr, maghrib and isha times live, based on data from the Presidency of Religious Affairs of Türkiye (Diyanet).

🧭

Qibla Compass

Easily find the direction of the Kaaba from where you are with a real-time compass. Distance included.

📅

Imsakiye Calendar

View monthly prayer times for the whole year. Supports both the Hijri and Gregorian calendars.

📖

The Holy Quran

The Arabic text and transliteration of all 114 surahs. Listen to recitations with a PRO membership.

✨

Asma al-Husna

The 99 beautiful names of Allah with their Arabic script, pronunciation and meanings. Includes search and audio playback.

🔔

Prayer Notifications

Set a separate notification for each prayer time and never miss a prayer.
