Privacy Policy

What happens to your information when you play Arrow Maze

Arrow Maze: Magic Puzzle ("the app") is made by Hasan Burak Koçyiğit ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

  • We do not collect or receive any personal data ourselves. The app has no accounts and no analytics.
  • From version 1.3 the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show them, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, and on iPhone and iPad it asks whether it may track you. Your answers never change the game.
  • The game works offline. Only ads and Apple's optional Game Center (from version 1.2) use the internet.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • your settings (language, sound, vibration, theme and accessibility options);
  • your progress (solved levels and your best results);
  • the level you are currently playing, so you can continue later;
  • how many levels you have won since the last ad and when it was shown, so that ads stay spaced out;
  • whether you have bought "Remove ads".

This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. The only exceptions are described in the Ads and Game Center sections below. You can erase your progress at any time in Settings › Reset progress. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Data we collect

None. We run no servers for the app and receive no data about you. AdMob only gives us totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned, which do not identify anyone. Apart from what Game Center shows every player on a leaderboard, we cannot see who plays the app or how.

Ads

From version 1.3 the app shows a full-screen ad between levels from time to time, never during play. After losing a level you can also choose to watch an ad to get one heart back. The ads come from Google AdMob, Google's ad service. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • your device's IP address, from which an approximate location (such as your country or city) is derived;
  • information about your device and the app, such as the device model, system version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking (see below);
  • diagnostic data about the ad software.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). How Google uses information from apps that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before the first ad. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes, and change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy options. Where a similar law applies to you elsewhere, the same option appears in Settings.
  • Tracking: on iPhone and iPad, the system asks once, before the first ad, whether the app may track you. If you allow it, ads can be personalised using your device's advertising identifier. If you do not, the identifier is not shared and the ads you see are not personalised. You can change this at any time in your device settings (Privacy & Security › Tracking).
  • Remove ads: a one-time in-app purchase turns off the full-screen ads between levels. The optional ad for a heart stays available.
  • Without an internet connection no ads are shown, and the game plays the same.

In-app purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through Apple's App Store, and Apple handles the payment. We never receive your payment details or your Apple ID. The app only learns from the App Store whether you have made the purchase, so that it can switch the ads off; "Restore purchases" in Settings asks the App Store again, for example on a new device.

Game Center

Game Center is optional and available from version 1.2 of the app. If you are signed in to it on your device, the app sends your achievement progress and your leaderboard scores (counts of solved levels, calculated from your saved progress) to Apple. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent and the game plays the same.

Apple processes this data under its own privacy policy. Depending on your Game Center settings, leaderboards can show your Game Center name and score to other players. You can sign out or change these settings at any time in your device settings. Resetting your progress in the app does not remove achievements you have already earned in Game Center.

Crash reports and device backups

If you have allowed it in your device settings, Apple (or Google on Android) may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. They do not identify you and are covered by Apple's or Google's own privacy policies. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings.

Your device's own backup (for example iCloud) may include the app's saved data, depending on your settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children. Players who do not allow tracking, and players in the regions above who do not give consent, see ads that are not personalised.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete: the data on your device is fully under your control. Data collected for ads is held by Google; you can exercise your rights with Google and change your ad choices as described above. You may still contact us with any question about your privacy, including under laws such as the GDPR or Turkey's personal data protection law (KVKK).

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds another feature that processes data, we will update this policy and the app's privacy information in the store before that version is released. The date below shows the latest version.
