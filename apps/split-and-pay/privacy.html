Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it

1. Overview

Split and Pay ("the App") lets you record shared expenses and keep track of balances with friends and groups. Doing that requires an account and a server, so unlike a purely offline app, some of your data is stored outside your device. This policy explains exactly what that data is, who can see it, and how to remove it.

The App is developed and operated by Burak Koçyiğit ("we"). You can reach us at any time at burakkocyigit1@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

a) Account Information

When you sign up we collect your full name, e-mail address and phone number. The phone number is required and must be unique, because it is one of the ways other users find and add you. You may also add a profile photo. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is stored only in hashed form — we never see or store it in plain text.

b) Content You Create

The App stores the records you and the people you share with create:

  • Groups you create or join, and their member lists
  • Expenses: description, amount, currency, category, date, optional note and optional receipt photo
  • How each expense is split between people, and whether each share has been settled
  • Settlement (payment) records between users
  • An activity history of these events, and the resulting notifications

c) Preferences and Technical Data

We store your preferred currency, time zone, app language, notification preferences, and a personal QR code identifier used for adding friends. If you enable notifications, we store a push notification token for your device so messages can be delivered to it.

d) Contacts — Read on Your Device Only

If you grant contacts permission, the App reads your device contact list locally, on your device, to help you find people to invite. Your contact list is not uploaded to our servers, not stored, and not shared with anyone. Revoking the permission in your device settings stops this access immediately; the rest of the App keeps working.

e) Camera and Photo Library

Camera access is used for two things: scanning another user's QR code to add them as a friend, and photographing a receipt. Photo library access is used when you attach an existing image instead. The App only accesses the specific image you choose — it does not scan or index your library.

f) Payment Information

We do not collect, process or store any payment card or bank account information. Split and Pay records who owes what; it does not move money. Any actual payment between you and another person happens outside the App.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and maintain your account and let others find you
  • To calculate and display balances between you, your friends and your groups
  • To show the shared activity history to the people involved in an expense
  • To send push notifications about events that concern you
  • To keep your preferences (currency, language, time zone) consistent across sessions
  • To diagnose errors and improve the App's reliability

We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and the App contains no advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

4. Who Can See Your Data

Shared expense tracking only works if the people involved can see the same records. Access is therefore limited as follows:

  • Your name and profile photo are visible to users you are connected to — your friends and the members of your groups
  • An expense and its split are visible to the people included in that expense and, for group expenses, to the members of that group
  • When a friendship or group membership ends, your profile is no longer visible to that user going forward

Names already recorded in past activity entries are stored as a snapshot at the time of the event and are not retroactively removed, so that historical records stay readable to the people who were part of them.

5. Receipt and Group Photos

Images you attach — receipt photos and group photos — are uploaded to our storage provider. Please note that these images are served from publicly reachable URLs: the links are long and unguessable and are not listed anywhere, but anyone who obtains a link can open the image without signing in. Please avoid attaching photos containing sensitive personal information. Deleting the related expense or group removes the image reference from the App.

6. Third-Party Services

a) Supabase

We use Supabase for authentication, database and file storage. Your account and the records described above are stored on Supabase infrastructure. See the Supabase Privacy Policy.

b) Expo Push Notification Service

Push notifications are delivered through Expo's notification service, which relays them to Apple and Google. The message content and your device push token pass through this service. See the Expo Privacy Policy.

c) App Stores

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Their own privacy policies apply to the download and installation process.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and its data at any time by writing to burakkocyigit1@gmail.com. When an account is deleted, the profile, expenses and splits belonging to it are removed.

One limitation is worth stating plainly: records that are shared with other people — for example an expense that also includes a friend, or an activity entry in their history — may remain visible to those users, because the record is also theirs. We remove your personal profile data; we cannot erase another person's copy of a shared financial record.

8. Security

  • All communication between the App and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS connections
  • Access to every record is enforced at the database level, so a user cannot read data they are not a party to
  • Passwords are stored only as hashes, never in plain text
  • Session credentials are kept in the device's secure storage
  • Your device contact list never leaves your device

No system is perfectly secure, but we take these measures seriously and fix issues promptly when they are reported.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and request a copy
  • Correct your name, e-mail, phone number or profile photo from within the App
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Withdraw contacts, camera, photo or notification permissions at any time from your device settings
  • Object to how your data is processed

To exercise any of these, write to burakkocyigit1@gmail.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the App evolves. Significant changes will be announced within the App. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
